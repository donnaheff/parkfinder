# ParkSwift Investor Pitch

## One-liner

**ParkSwift helps drivers find, reserve, and pay for parking before arrival, reducing traffic congestion, wasted fuel, and parking frustration in fast-growing cities.**

## Vision

Start as a smart parking finder in Lagos. Grow into a sovereign mobility infrastructure platform for parking, curb management, EV charging, permits, events, enforcement workflows, data exchange, and city-level mobility governance.

## Problem

Urban drivers waste time searching for parking. This creates avoidable congestion, fuel wastage, user frustration, and lost productivity. Parking operators also lack modern tools for live availability, payments, pricing, customer acquisition, and analytics.

## Solution

ParkSwift is a mobile-friendly parking marketplace that lets users:

- Search nearby parking spaces
- View live availability
- Compare price, distance, accessibility, EV support, and walking distance
- Reserve and pay before arrival
- Use Free or Premium features
- Reduce circling time, congestion, and fuel wastage

## Product Phases

### Phase 0 — MVP Foundation

- Mobile-friendly app
- Live parking spaces
- Map-based discovery
- Mobile reservation and payment
- Parking lot search and filters
- Congestion-reduction positioning

### Phase 1 — Commercial Tiering

- Free tier
- Premium tier
- Premium AI pick
- Heatmaps
- Price alerts

### Later Phases

- IoT sensor integrations
- Operator dashboard
- Permits
- Accessibility routing
- Enforcement workflows

- Privacy analytics
- Vehicle-to-infrastructure handoff
- Curb management
- Carbon reporting

## Business Model

1. Booking/reservation fees
2. Premium commuter subscription
3. Parking operator SaaS
4. Corporate parking wallets
5. Payment revenue share where allowed
6. Future API marketplace
7. Future city mobility analytics and reporting
8. Future permits and curb-management contracts

## Target Launch Market

Initial city: **Lagos, Nigeria**

Initial zones:

- Victoria Island
- Ikoyi
- Lagos Island/Marina
- Lekki Phase 1
- Yaba
- Ikeja
- Event and mall corridors

## Why Now

Cities are becoming more congested, vehicles are becoming more connected, and users expect mobile-first payments and navigation. Smart parking is a practical entry point into a larger urban mobility platform.

## Funding Scenarios

### Lean MVP: ₦45m–₦65m

Best for a limited prototype with minimal real-world integrations and founder-heavy execution.

### Recommended Pilot: ₦95m–₦155m

Best for a serious 5–6 month commercial pilot with operator onboarding, payments, marketing, and operational support.

### Accelerated City Pilot: ₦180m–₦250m

Best if the team wants to include real sensors, stronger field operations, more integrations, and multiple launch zones immediately.
