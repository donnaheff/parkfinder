Phased roadmap

ParkSwift implementation phases.

This roadmap organizes everything from our conversation into practical delivery phases: MVP parking finder, premium features, smart-city modules, next-stage mobility, sovereign infrastructure, and national rollout.

Open app Module hub Investor pitch Manage lots
Phase 0

Concept and MVP foundation

Build the basic smart parking finder experience requested at the start.

  • Mobile-friendly parking finder app
  • Live parking-space display
  • Satellite-style map view
  • Mobile parking payment and reservation
  • Congestion and fuel-waste reduction messaging

Status: implemented in index.html, map.html, lots.html, and checkout.html.

Phase 1

Multi-page app and commercial tiers

Split the long page into a structured product and introduce Free/Premium logic.

  • Dashboard, map, lots, checkout, pricing, and modules pages
  • Free tier with basic discovery, route, payment, and 3-hour booking
  • Premium tier with AI pick, heatmap, priority hold, price alerts, and 24-hour booking
  • Local browser state for selected tier and bookings

Status: implemented and enabled.

Phase 2

Core smart-city operating modules

Turn the parking app into a broader parking operations platform.

  • Verified IoT feeds and sensor gateway
  • Predictive availability
  • Operator dashboard
  • Digital permits
  • Accessibility routing
  • Enforcement workflows
  • Corporate accounts
  • Privacy analytics

Status: implemented in separate module pages and enabled.

Phase 3

Next-stage connected mobility

Extend the platform beyond traditional parking into curb, vehicles, events, APIs, carbon, and security.

  • Vehicle-to-infrastructure parking handoff
  • Dynamic curb management
  • Event surge planning
  • Carbon reporting
  • Open API marketplace
  • Fraud and spoofing defense

Status: implemented but disabled by .env feature flags.

Phase 4

Sovereign foundation layer

Add the controls required for national digital sovereignty and public-sector trust.

  • National mobility data exchange
  • Local data residency enforcement
  • National digital identity integration
  • Privacy-preserving identity
  • Domestic payment rails
  • Local cryptographic key custody
  • Open standards compliance
  • National parking registry
  • Sovereign map layer
  • AI governance and transparent pricing

Status: implemented in data-governance.html but disabled by .env.

Phase 5

Public governance and resilience

Give cities, regulators, and citizens safe governance tools.

  • Public-sector control plane
  • Offline-first parking operations
  • Emergency and resilience mode
  • Regulatory reporting dashboard
  • Citizen transparency portal
  • Complaint and dispute resolution
  • Interoperable enforcement governance

Status: implemented across sovereign suite pages but disabled by .env.

Phase 6

Local ecosystem and procurement readiness

Prepare the platform for local market participation, public procurement, and responsible innovation.

  • Local ecosystem marketplace
  • Governed data monetization
  • Sovereign procurement readiness
  • National mobility sandbox
  • Security operations center

Status: implemented in ecosystem.html and enforcement-governance.html, disabled by .env.

Phase 7

Vertical products and inclusion

Package the platform for specific sectors and make it usable for more people.

  • EV charging integration
  • Logistics and delivery integration
  • Campus, airport, hospital, mall, and stadium editions
  • Local language support
  • SMS, USSD, low-data, and voice guidance

Status: implemented in verticals.html, disabled by .env.

Phase 8

Federation and national rollout

Scale from one city prototype into a federated national mobility infrastructure platform.

  • Multi-city federation
  • Sovereign mobility operating system
  • Long-term sovereign architecture
  • Cross-city accounts with city-level data boundaries
  • National reporting with privacy-safe aggregation

Status: implemented in mobility-os.html, disabled by .env.

How feature flags fit the phases

The current prototype keeps later phases disabled by default. To activate a disabled phase, enable its flags in smart-parking-finder/.env and regenerate assets/env.js.

cd smart-parking-finder
# edit .env and change selected FEATURE_* values to true
node scripts/sync-env.js
# refresh the browser
