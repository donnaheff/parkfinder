Pre-seed investor pitch

ParkSwift: find, reserve, and pay for parking before arrival.

A smart parking finder for African cities, starting with Lagos: live availability, mobile reservation, payments, premium routing, and a roadmap toward sovereign mobility infrastructure.

Watch promo GIFWatch product demo GIFOpen prototype

The problem

Drivers lose time, fuel, and patience looking for parking.

  • Urban drivers circle streets searching for available spaces.
  • Parking operators lack real-time digital demand visibility.
  • Cities lose road capacity to avoidable cruising and congestion.
  • Payments, permits, enforcement, and data are fragmented.
10–20 min

Typical avoidable search-time target ParkSwift aims to reduce per parking trip during peak periods.

₦

Revenue opportunity across booking fees, subscriptions, operator SaaS, payments, fleet accounts, and data products.

The solution

A mobile-first parking marketplace with live availability and payments.

  • Search nearby parking by destination, vehicle type, and walking distance.
  • See availability, price, distance, accessibility, EV, and congestion signals.
  • Reserve and pay before arrival.
  • Use Free for basic discovery or Premium for heatmaps, AI pick, alerts, and priority holds.

Product phases

Start with Phase 0 and Phase 1. Expand into sovereign mobility later.

Phase 0

MVP parking finder

Mobile app, live spaces, map, reservations, and payment.

Phase 1

Commercial tiers

Free/Premium features, revenue model, tier gating, and launch-ready UX.

Later

Sovereign platform

IoT, operators, permits, data exchange, public control plane, and federation.

Business model

Multiple revenue lines from day one.

  • Consumer reservation/service fee on successful bookings.
  • Premium subscription for commuters and power users.
  • Operator SaaS dashboard for parking lot owners.
  • Corporate fleet wallets and monthly invoices.
  • Future API, city, permit, curb, and analytics revenue.

Phase 0–1 commencement budget

Recommended starting capital: ₦125 million.

Recommended range: ₦95 million–₦155 million for a 5–6 month Lagos pilot. A lean prototype can start lower, but ₦125 million gives enough room for product, integrations, pilot operations, marketing, and contingency.

Investment ask

Raise ₦125m to launch the first commercial pilot.

Use of funds: build a production-grade Phase 0–1 product, onboard the first parking operators, validate payments and premium conversion, and collect data for the next fundraising round.

  • 5–6 month runway for Phase 0 and Phase 1.
  • Target first city: Lagos.
  • Initial focus: high-density business, mall, campus, and event zones.
  • Milestone for next round: active parking supply, repeat users, completed reservations, and operator revenue.
